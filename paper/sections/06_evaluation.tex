\section{Evaluation}
\label{sec:eval}

We answer five questions:
\textbf{(Q1)} How does dLLM inference scale with context compared with AR models of identical
architecture?
\textbf{(Q2)} How well do prefill and decode scale across nodes?
\textbf{(Q3)} What does each \sys technique contribute?
\textbf{(Q4)} What is the largest context \sys can serve, and at what quality?
\textbf{(Q5)} Do the techniques generalize across models, GPU generations and NICs?

\textbf{Baselines.} All systems run on SGLang: (1) SGLang dLLM as released (LowConfidence /
JointThreshold, FDFO, TP/EP); (2) AR models on SGLang with decode context parallelism, MoRI-EP and
MoRI prefill/decode disaggregation; (3) published dInfer~\cite{ma2025dinfer} and
Fast-dLLM~\cite{wu2025fastdllm} numbers, labeled as cross-platform references.
Component baselines: RCCL all-to-all versus MoRI-EP kernels; MoRI-IO (plain, chunked, multi-QP)
versus shard-aligned versus IBGDA streaming; NCCL-style DCP merge versus MoRI merge; UEC versus
RoCEv2 transport; BF16 versus FP8 KV.

\textbf{Metrics.} Time to first token (prefill), per-step latency, \TPF, decode tokens/s per request,
goodput under SLOs, tokens/s/GPU, energy per token, peak memory, and NIAH/RULER accuracy.
